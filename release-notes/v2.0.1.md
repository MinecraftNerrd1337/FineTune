## Fixes

- **AirPods Max over USB-C**: with the cable plugged in and Bluetooth on, every app went silent while FineTune ran. They now play over the cable as expected (upstream issue #491).
- **Apps paused during a device change**: an app that stopped its sound for a moment while the output changed, for instance as AirPods connect, stayed on the old output until you switched by hand. It now follows the new output.
- **No loud burst when switching outputs**: switching to a device with an EQ preset played its first moments without the preset's preamp. The preset now applies from the first sample.

## New

- **Keep Volume During Calls**: FaceTime and other calls no longer turn your other apps down to about 18%. On by default, in Settings → Audio. Approach from CharlieBaker2002's upstream PR #482.
- **HUD display**: choose whether the volume HUD shows on the active display or on the main display, in Settings → Shortcuts → Volume HUD.

## Small changes

- The About tab shows the version without the build number.
